# Introducing Voice Share

Customer journeys today involve overlapping use of multiple channels. **Voice Share** exploits the ubiquity of the web towards fantastic call experiences.

Voice Share(Voice) employs multi-device; live content sharing technology, customer analytics and brand specific visitor side experiences. It enables the voice agents to:

- Initiate an online session with the caller.
- Share content and apps over a wide range of customer smartphones, tablets, and desktops—during the call and/or at the end of it (no downloads required).
- Save time while disposing the calls in CRM, using the summary auto-generated in Voice Share.
